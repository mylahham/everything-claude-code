Symptoms and clinical course

A slow loss — from the feet upward

  1. ≈ 3 years

    Clumsy walker

    • Unsteady gait, falls
    • Weak feet, high arches
    • Tightly curled hair
  2. 5–10 years

    Peripheral nerves

    • Lost reflexes and sensation
    • Muscle wasting
    • Biopsy: giant axons
  3. Adolescence

    Brain involved

    • Wheelchair, ataxia
    • Slurred speech, nystagmus
    • Scoliosis, seizures
  4. 20s

    Late stage

    • Swallowing, breathing fail
    • Respiratory failure
